# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Small business owners, founders, and marketers who need a landing page, small site, or app fast and are evaluating whether to hire Precious. They're comparing him against agencies and other freelancers, often on a deadline, and want proof of speed, reliability, and taste before committing money. They land here from a referral or social link, skim in under a minute, and decide whether to book a call or bounce.

## Product Purpose

SIGNAL LABS is Precious Opara's freelance/solo-agency portfolio site. It sells fixed-price, fast-turnaround web work (72-hour landing pages, small multi-page sites, and small apps) to founders and small businesses worldwide. Success is a visitor believing "this person is fast, skilled, and won't waste my time" and booking a call or sending a brief.

## Brand Personality

Warm & approachable craftsman. Human and personal rather than corporate-agency; feels like a skilled individual you'd trust with your project, not a templated SaaS machine. Confident about the 72-hour/fixed-price promise without being loud or gimmicky. Should read as made-by-a-person, not generated.

## Anti-references

The current build is textbook "blue-ish AI slop": violet/coral/mint tri-color gradient reused everywhere (text, buttons, borders, avatars), `background-clip: text` gradient headlines, glassy blurred nav, identical rounded (18-20px) card grids for work/pricing/process/testimonials, pill-shaped mono-font eyebrows, ghost-card border+soft-shadow combos, ambient aurora blur backgrounds. None of this should survive — it should stop being recognizable as an AI-generated portfolio template at a glance.

## Design Principles

- Prove speed and craft through the interface itself, not just the copy — the site's own polish is the pitch.
- One human hand, not a component library — deliberate, slightly irregular choices over symmetric, interchangeable card grids.
- Confidence without noise — strong typography and a committed but restrained palette carry the page; skip gradient/glow crutches.
- Every section earns its own layout — no repeating the same card-grid reflex site-wide.
- Fast in feel as well as fact — motion and load should reinforce the 72-hour promise, not fight it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast, large text ≥3:1. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (existing reduced-motion handling should be preserved and extended to any new motion). Keyboard-navigable nav, forms, and FAQ accordion with visible focus states.
