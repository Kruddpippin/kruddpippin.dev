# Care Plan has no proof point

The `Care Plan` package ($50/mo, "unlimited small edits") in [config.js](../../src/data/config.js) is the only recurring line item, but nothing on the page demonstrates what it actually looks like in practice for a client — no example edit, no mention in testimonials.

## Why it matters

It's the highest-margin, lowest-friction upsell on the page (per [[DESIGN]] it's a full-width strip, not a competing tier) but it's asking for recurring trust with zero evidence, unlike the one-time tiers which are backed by the whole Work grid.

## Fix

Low priority. If it isn't converting, pair it with a one-line example ("last month: 6 edits, 2 fixes, zero downtime") or fold a Care Plan mention into a future testimonial.
