# No organic content channel

There's no blog, case-study series, or any content route on the site — it's a single landing page.

## Why it matters

[[PRODUCT]] states the visitor "land[s] here from a referral or social link" — that's the only acquisition channel the site is designed for. That's a legitimate strategy for a fast-turnaround freelance offer, not a bug, but it's a real ceiling: no organic-search path, no compounding asset. Worth a deliberate "not now" rather than an unnoticed gap.

## Fix

Not urgent. If growth stalls on referrals alone, the cheapest next step is 2-3 short case-study writeups of shipped projects (reuses the existing `projects` screenshots), not a general blog.
